# Feature Specification: Structured Tax Summary Report & Tax Profile Dashboard (FR16.4)

**Feature ID:** FR16.4  
**Status:** 📝 Planned  
**Title:** Structured Tax Summary Report & Old vs New Regime Comparison  
**GitHub Issue:** [#519](https://github.com/aashishbhanawat/ArthSaarthi/issues/519)  
**User Story:** As a user planning my annual taxes, I want a consolidated Financial Year Tax Readiness summary report, so that I can view my overall tax profile, gross income, TDS credits, eligible deductions, capital gains, and estimated net tax liability under Old vs New Tax Regimes.

---

## 1. Sub-Task Breakdown & Implementation Modules

1. **Module 1.1 (Backend Aggregator & Tax Engine):** Aggregation pipeline compiling Income (FR16.1/16.2), Deductions (FR16.3), Capital Gains (FR6.5), Dividends (FR6.6), and Old vs New Regime (Section 115BAC) math engine (`TaxRegimeService`).
2. **Module 1.2 (Report Exporters):** PDF and CSV structured report exporters including mandatory legal disclaimer.
3. **Module 1.3 (Backend API):** Endpoint `GET /api/v1/tax/summary` and export routes.
4. **Module 1.4 (Frontend UI):** Build `TaxSummaryDashboard.tsx`, Old vs New Regime comparison card, and export modal.

---

## 2. Backend & Frontend Responsibilities

* **Backend (`backend/app/services/tax_regime_service.py`):**
  - Consolidate income, TDS, deductions, realized gains, and dividend streams.
  - Calculate tax under Old Tax Regime (progressive slabs + exemptions + 4% Cess).
  - Calculate tax under New Tax Regime (Section 115BAC slabs + ₹75k standard deduction + 87A rebate + 4% Cess).
  - Generate PDF/CSV reports.
* **Frontend (`frontend/src/pages/TaxSummaryDashboard.tsx` & `RegimeComparisonCard.tsx`):**
  - Render side-by-side Old vs New Tax Regime comparison.
  - Display non-advisory legal notice banner.
  - Export PDF/CSV trigger modals.
  - Privacy Mode currency masking (`usePrivacySensitiveCurrency`).

---

## 3. Test Plan & Corner Cases

### 3.1. Corner Cases
* **Zero Taxable Income (Section 87A Rebate):** Under New Regime, net taxable income up to ₹7,00,000 / ₹7,50,000 triggers 87A rebate resulting in ₹0 tax liability.
* **High Income Surcharge Slabs:** Total income exceeding ₹50L, ₹1Cr, or ₹2Cr correctly applies statutory tax surcharge rates.
* **Exempt Dividend / Intraday Income:** Handles dividend and intraday capital gains tax classification correctly.

### 3.2. Manual Testing Matrix
* [ ] Verify Old vs New Regime side-by-side values match manual CA tax computations.
* [ ] Confirm lower tax regime is highlighted with "Recommended Regime" badge.
* [ ] Generate PDF & CSV export and verify mandatory disclaimer text presence.
* [ ] Test Privacy Mode toggling.

## 5. UX & UI Specifications

* **Comparison Cards:** Side-by-side dual card layout comparing Old Regime vs New Regime.
* **Legal Disclaimer Banner:** Mandatory non-advisory disclaimer banner displayed at the top of the report and in exports:
  > *Disclaimer: This report is generated for informational tax planning purposes only and does not constitute formal financial or tax advice.*

---

## 6. Database Schema & Security Encryption (`EncryptedString`)

* Aggregates data from encrypted `IncomeEntry`, `TaxDeduction`, `Transaction`, and `Holding` tables.
* Decrypts values dynamically in memory for tax calculation and report generation without persisting unencrypted temporary files.

# Release Notes - v1.3.0

ArthSaarthi v1.3.0 introduces major enhancements across Financial Goal Analytics, Upstox V3 Market Data Integration, Grable & Lytton Risk Profiling, Android Background Portfolio Sync, and Multi-Tenant Security Isolation.

## Key Highlights

### 1. Financial Goal Projections & Required SIP Calculator (FR13.3 & FR13.4)
Take control of your long-term wealth goals:
- **SIP Calculator:** Calculates required monthly SIP contributions using ordinary annuity formulas, accounting for target duration, asset compounding, and fallback return rates.
- **Dynamic Growth Path:** Compiles unified XIRR across linked assets and plots target vs projected growth paths on an interactive chart.
- **Track Status Badges:** Automatically marks goals as **On Track** or **Off Track**.

### 2. Upstox V3 Market Data & Public Asset Seeding (#498)
- **Zero-Cost Market Data:** Queries public unauthenticated Upstox V3 historical candles and V2 market trading holidays API with rate limiting.
- **Asset Seeding from CDN:** Automatically downloads and caches `NSE.json.gz` from Upstox CDN for 0-cost $O(1)$ ISIN $\leftrightarrow$ Symbol lookups.

### 3. Grable & Lytton 13-Question Risk Scale (#481)
- **Comprehensive Risk Assessment:** Multi-step questionnaire (13 questions, up to 47 points) classifying risk profiles into Conservative, Moderate, Growth, or Aggressive.
- **Target Asset Allocation:** Visual breakdown of recommended equity, debt, gold, and liquid portfolio distribution.

### 4. Android Background Sync & Mobile Enablement (#492, #493, #494)
- **WorkManager Background Sync:** Native `SnapshotWorker.kt` automatically executes daily portfolio snapshot valuations.
- **Native Settings Toggle:** Profile page includes `AndroidSettingsCard` for toggling background sync.
- **Embedded Python Stabilization:** Fully compatible SQLite/DiskCache layer for Chaquopy Android runtime.

### 5. Multi-Tenant Security & Tenant Isolation
- **Tax Reports Security:** Enforced strict user-level authorization filtering on Capital Gains and Dividend endpoints (#423).
- **Goal Links & PPF Isolation:** IDOR protections on goal asset links and user-scoped PPF account tickers (`PPF-{user_id_short}-{account_number}`).
- **PPF Immutability:** System-generated PPF interest credit transactions are now immutable.

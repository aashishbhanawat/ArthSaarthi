# Release Scope & Requirements - v1.4.0 (Tax Readiness & Full Financial Picture)

ArthSaarthi v1.4.0 focuses on **Tax Readiness & Full Financial Picture**, equipping the platform with essential income tracking, tax deduction management, unrealized capital gains analytics, and consolidated tax readiness reporting. This lays the groundwork for AI tax-loss harvesting and tax optimization in future releases.

> [!NOTE]
> For the complete 11-point architectural blueprint, database encryption model, test automation matrix, and Chaquopy Android compatibility guide, see [`docs/v1.4.0_detailed_plan.md`](file:///media/data/AppData/CodeServer/pms4/ArthSaarthi/docs/v1.4.0_detailed_plan.md).

*   **Tracking File:** [`docs/issues/42_calculate_unrealized_capital_gains.md`](file:///media/data/AppData/CodeServer/pms4/ArthSaarthi/docs/issues/42_calculate_unrealized_capital_gains.md)
